SAML inspector

Paste a SAML message in any form: a redirect URL, a POST body, a raw HTTP request, base64 (deflated or not), or XML. It is decoded in your browser with WebAssembly; nothing you paste is sent to the server.

Check signatures against a certificate

Every XML signature is checked with the certificate in its own KeyInfo anyway, which shows whether the message was changed. Only a certificate you trust, such as the one in the issuer's metadata, shows who signed it.
