Inspector

Decode a SAML message in any form: a redirect URL, a POST body, a raw HTTP request, base64 (deflated or not), or XML. What you paste doesn't leave your browser.

Check signatures against a certificate, or decrypt with a key

Every XML signature is checked with the certificate in its own KeyInfo anyway, which shows whether the message was changed. Only a certificate you trust, such as the one in the issuer's metadata, shows who signed it. A private key decrypts encrypted assertions; like the message, it stays in your browser.
