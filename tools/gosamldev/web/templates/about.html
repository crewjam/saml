About

gosaml.dev is a set of SAML 2.0 testing tools built on crewjam/saml, a SAML implementation in Go.

The test IdPs and SPs use the library's x/samlidp and x/samlsp packages with their default checks, so they behave as an application built on it would.

Everything is temporary

Test IdPs and SPs, their keys, users and logs are deleted {{.Lifetime}} after they are created. Until then the server keeps them in a database on its own disk, which outlives restarts and deploys. Nothing is backed up.

Who can see what

Management page
Anyone who has its URL, and no one else. Keep it to yourself.
Metadata and endpoints
Public, as SAML needs them to be.
Logs
An instance's log holds the messages it exchanged, with the HTTP requests they came in, including the attributes of users who logged in through it. Cookie values are shortened. Do not use real accounts or personal data.
Sessions
Kept in the same temporary database, with a cookie in your browser, for single sign-on and single logout.
The inspector
Decodes in your browser, keys included. What you paste is not sent anywhere. A Response an IdP posts to the inspector page does reach the server, which puts it in the page and keeps nothing.
